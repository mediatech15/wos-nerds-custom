Whiteout Survival Tools (WOS Nerds Custom)

About

This a custom version of the WOS Nerds project, created by a state looking to help their players coordinate better and win more. We are not affiliated with the original WOS Nerds project, but we are grateful for their work and inspiration.

Available Tools

Rally Planner

Coordinate rally timings and manage march times for optimal coordination.

Troop Stats

Compare troop types and their effectiveness in battle.

Layout Planner

Plan your base layout or castle battle layout.
